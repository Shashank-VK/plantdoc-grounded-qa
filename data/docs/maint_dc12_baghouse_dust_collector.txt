1. PULSE-JET CLEANING PARAMETERS AND COMPRESSED AIR SUPPLY
Baghouse DC-12 contains 480 filter bags arranged in 16 rows cleaned by reverse pulse-jet compressed air valves PV-01 through PV-16.
Cleaning air supply reservoir pressure must be maintained strictly between 5.5 bar and 6.0 bar dry air.
Pulse firing duration timer is calibrated to 120 milliseconds per valve firing sequence.
The sequential cleaning controller initiates on demand when tube sheet differential pressure across bags exceeds 1500 Pa (15.0 mbar).
Normal clean-filter operating differential pressure range is 800 Pa to 1200 Pa.

2. FILTER MEDIA REPLACEMENT CRITERIA AND CAGE CHECKS
Filter bags are fabricated from PTFE-membrane laminated needle-punched fiberglass felt (2.0 mm thickness, rating 260°C continuous).
Routine replacement of all 480 filter bags is mandated every 18 months or earlier if baseline clean differential pressure exceeds 1800 Pa.
During bag changeout, each internal support cage (12-wire galvanized carbon steel) must be inspected for corrosion, burrs, or broken rings.
Fluorescent dye powder test (using black light UV inspection in clean air plenum) must be conducted post-replacement to detect pinhole leaks.

3. HOPPER ASH LEVEL AND ROTARY AIRLOCK DISCHARGE
Dust collected on exterior bag surfaces falls into hopper HOP-12 equipped with bin aerators and heating pads.
Hopper rotary paddle level switch LSH-12 alarms when collected ash reaches 70% hopper storage capacity.
Rotary airlock valve RAV-12 operates at fixed speed 15 RPM to discharge ash into dense-phase pneumatic conveying vessel PV-10.
Hopper wall heating pads must maintain temperature above 135°C to prevent acid dew point condensation and bag blinding.
