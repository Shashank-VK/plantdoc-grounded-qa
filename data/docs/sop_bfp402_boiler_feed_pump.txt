STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-MEC-402
EQUIPMENT: Boiler Feed Pump BFP-402 (High-Pressure Multi-Stage Centrifugal)
REVISION: 4.1 | EFFECTIVE DATE: 2025-11-15 | LOCATION: Power & Utilities Area B

1. PRE-STARTUP INSPECTION AND PERMISSIVES
Before initiating the electric motor drive for BFP-402, the shift technician must verify mechanical seal integrity and auxiliary lube oil supply.
The auxiliary lube oil skid must be started in manual mode. Verify lube oil delivery pressure is at least 1.8 bar (normal operating range 2.2 to 2.5 bar).
If lube oil pressure fails to reach 1.8 bar within 45 seconds of pump start, the permissive interlock will reject the startup sequence.
Inspect the mechanical seal flush line for continuous flow of demineralized water at a minimum rate of 12 L/min and inlet pressure of 4.0 bar.

2. VALVE ALIGNMENT AND SUCTION CHECKS
Confirm suction isolation valve BFP-V-101 is fully open and locked with mechanical car-seal.
Never throttle the suction valve under any operational circumstances to prevent cavitation and impeller erosion.
Check suction pressure gauge PI-402A; minimum required suction head is 3.5 barg deaerator static head.
Discharge minimum flow recirculation valve FCV-402 must be set to AUTO mode (minimum recirculation flow setpoint 85 m3/h).

3. VIBRATION AND THERMAL TRIP LIMITS
During steady-state operation at 2980 RPM, radial bearing vibration velocity must remain below 4.5 mm/s RMS (Alarm threshold).
A vibration level exceeding 7.1 mm/s RMS sustained for greater than 3 seconds will trigger an automatic emergency trip of breaker 52-BFP402.
Radial bearing metal temperatures (TE-402-1 through TE-402-4) must remain below 80°C.
If bearing temperature exceeds 90°C, the operator must trip the unit manually within 60 seconds if automatic trip fails.
