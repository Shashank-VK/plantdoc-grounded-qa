1. INSULATING OIL DIELECTRIC PROPERTIES AND TEST THRESHOLDS
Transformer TR-04 is filled with 14,500 Liters of uninhibited mineral insulating oil conforming to IEC 60296.
Dielectric breakdown voltage (measured across 2.5 mm spherical electrodes per IEC 60156) must exceed 50.0 kV for in-service oil.
If dielectric breakdown voltage drops below 40.0 kV, the transformer oil must undergo high-vacuum filtration and dehydration immediately.
Moisture content in oil must remain below 15 ppm at standard operating temperature.
Dielectric dissipation factor (tan delta at 90°C) must not exceed 0.005.

2. DISSOLVED GAS ANALYSIS (DGA) ACTION LIMITS
Routine quarterly oil sampling is sent to the analytical lab for Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) by gas chromatography.
Acetylene (C2H2) concentration must remain below 2.0 ppm; any value above 5.0 ppm indicates active high-energy arcing requiring emergency shutdown.
Hydrogen (H2) baseline limit is 100 ppm; values between 100 ppm and 300 ppm require bi-weekly monitoring for partial discharge.
Carbon monoxide (CO) to carbon dioxide (CO2) ratio below 0.10 indicates thermal degradation of cellulosic paper insulation.
Total combustible gas (TCG) generation rate exceeding 10 ppm per day triggers immediate root-cause investigation.

3. TEMPERATURE MONITORING AND COOLING CONTROLS
Transformer TR-04 uses ONAN/ONAF cooling with two redundant fan cooling banks (Bank A and Bank B).
Top oil temperature alarm activates at 85°C; emergency trip activates at 95°C.
Winding hot-spot temperature indicator (WTI-401) initiates cooling fans at 65°C and asserts trip contact at 105°C.
Silica gel desiccant in the conservator tank breather must be replaced whenever more than 60% of the crystal volume turns pink.
