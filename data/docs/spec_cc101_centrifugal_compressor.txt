1. LUBRICATING OIL REQUIREMENTS AND RESERVOIR CAPACITY
Compressor CC-101 and its associated reduction gearbox GB-101 use ISO VG 46 synthetic polyalphaolefin (PAO) turbine lubricating oil.
The main lubricating oil reservoir RES-101 has a total operational capacity of 450 Liters.
The minimum allowable oil level in the reservoir sight glass is 65% (approximately 290 Liters).
Oil supply pressure to journal bearings must be maintained between 2.0 bar and 2.4 bar at an oil cooler outlet temperature of 42°C to 48°C.
Oil replacement is required every 8000 operating hours or when kinematic viscosity deviates by more than +/- 10% from 46 cSt at 40°C.

2. DRY GAS SEAL SPECIFICATIONS AND PRESSURE DIFFERENTIAL
Dry gas seals on CC-101 utilize nitrogen buffer gas supplied from header N2-HDR-01.
Seal gas pressure differential across the primary seal face must be maintained at a minimum of +0.35 bar above process suction pressure.
Primary seal vent leakage rate must remain below 15.0 normal liters per minute (Nl/min).
If primary vent flow exceeds 25.0 Nl/min, alarm ALM-SEAL-101 is asserted and secondary containment seal backup initiates.

3. OPERATING LIMITS AND SURGE MARGIN
Maximum continuous speed for CC-101 is 11,400 RPM with normal operating speed at 10,850 RPM.
The anti-surge valve ASV-101 modulated controller requires a minimum 12% safety margin from the predicted surge limit line.
Maximum allowable journal bearing temperature is 85°C (warning alarm at 80°C).
Total axial shaft displacement must not exceed 0.25 mm from thrust collar datum position.
