1. EQUALIZATION BASIN RESIDENCE TIME AND FLOW EQUALIZATION
Raw process wastewater streams enter primary equalization basin EQ-101 prior to neutralization.
The minimum hydraulic retention time (HRT) in basin EQ-101 is 8.0 hours at peak hydraulic inflow of 150 m3/h.
Submersible agitators AG-101A and AG-101B must run continuously at 1450 RPM to prevent suspended solids settling.
If equalization tank liquid level exceeds 85%, auxiliary overflow sump P-OVER is dispatched to storm surge pond TK-900.

2. TWO-STAGE CHEMICAL NEUTRALIZATION CHEMISTRY
Neutralization takes place across two consecutive agitated reaction tanks (NT-101 and NT-102).
If incoming pH is below 6.0, dosing pumps DP-NAOH-1A/B meter 20% liquid sodium hydroxide (caustic soda) into NT-101.
If incoming pH is above 8.5, dosing pumps DP-HCL-1A/B meter 15% technical hydrochloric acid solution into NT-101.
Tank NT-102 acts as fine-trimming polishing stage; trim dosing rate is governed by PID controller maintaining pH at 7.2 to 7.8.
Lime slurry dosing (5% hydrated calcium hydroxide) is reserved as emergency backup during high sulfate load surges.

3. FINAL EFFLUENT DISCHARGE SPECIFICATIONS AND TESTING
Discharge flume outfall analyzer AIT-DISCH measures compliance parameters continuously prior to municipal river canal release.
Final discharge pH must strictly stay within regulatory statutory limits of 6.5 to 8.5.
Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD) must not exceed 250 mg/L (daily composite sample testing via spectrophotometer).
Total Suspended Solids (TSS) in final clarifier overflow must remain below 50 mg/L; turbidity exceeding 30 NTU automatically diverts discharge to recycle loop.
