1. PRE-WARMING AND BARRING GEAR ENGAGEMENT
Before high-pressure steam introduction to Steam Turbine ST-200, engage hydraulic turning gear (barring gear) to rotate the rotor at 45 RPM.
The barring gear must operate continuously for a minimum of 4.0 hours prior to admission of main steam to prevent thermal rotor bowing.
Gland steam sealing pressure must be established at 0.25 to 0.30 barg using auxiliary header steam before pulling surface condenser vacuum.
Maintain surface condenser vacuum at or below -0.88 barg (-88 kPa gauge) before tripping turning gear and rolling on steam.

2. STEAM QUALITY AND EXPANSION CRITERIA
Main inlet throttle steam temperature must exhibit a minimum of 50°C superheat above steam saturation temperature.
Rotor-to-casing differential expansion measured by eddy-current probes DE-201 and DE-202 must not exceed 3.2 mm positive or 1.5 mm negative.
If differential expansion reaches 2.8 mm during thermal soak, the turbine acceleration ramp must be put on hold immediately.
Exhaust hood casing temperature must not exceed 85°C; water spray desuperheater on condenser neck activates if temperature reaches 75°C.

3. GOVERNOR SPEED RAMPING AND TRIP CHECKS
Accelerate turbine rotor through critical speed resonance zones (1400 - 1800 RPM) at an accelerated ramp rate of 600 RPM/min.
Normal ramp rate outside resonance bands is 300 RPM/min up to synchronous rated speed of 3000 RPM (50 Hz).
Perform mechanical overspeed trip test during routine semi-annual safety audit; overspeed bolt must actuate at 3300 RPM (+/- 30 RPM, 110% of rated speed).
Emergency hand trip push-buttons (located at field station and DCS console) immediately de-energize emergency stop valves ESV-201 and ESV-202 within 0.15 seconds.
